## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a warm, grounded, and artisanal culinary identity rooted in Kerala’s confectionery traditions. It embraces honest craftsmanship, genuine human warmth, and community intimacy while consciously avoiding sterile corporate tech minimalism and faux-European luxury clichés.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on modern editorial tactility:
- **Warm Paper Foundations:** Sterile `#FFFFFF` canvases and harsh stark-black contrasts are completely avoided. Instead, surfaces replicate parchment, unbleached flour, and warm baked pastry tones.
- **Tonal Contrast:** The interface navigates naturally between soft cream backgrounds and deep, immersive berry-tinted plum and ink surfaces.
- **Approachable Craft:** Micro-copy, organic curves, and rich culinary gradients (spiced orange, pistachio, cocoa, and berry plum) invoke the sensory world of baking, offering equal resonance for everyday consumers and discerning B2B partners.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from pastry ingredients and artisanal kitchen elements:
- **Primary (`#432237`):** A concentrated, deep plum tone representing richness, quality, and visual grounding. It anchors core display headings, strong interactive buttons, and hero locator panels.
- **Secondary (`#DE8A4C`):** A roasted terracotta orange functioning as a high-heat interactive spark for accents, tags, dynamic highlights, and call-to-action treatments.
- **Tertiary (`#7C4667`):** A mellowed, soft plum bridging primary depths to lighter editorial labels, badges, and decorative accents.
- **Neutral (`#F7EFE4`):** A warm, comforting cream that forms the baseline canvas for the entire experience.

### Layering & Contrast Rules
1. **Never use pure grayscale:** Dark values must always be tinted with berry ink (`#2A1620`), and light values must maintain a cream base (`#FFFBF5` or `#F7EFE4`).
2. **Inverted Plum/Ink Containers:** When rendering components on dark plum or ink canvases, foreground text and subtle borders must use specified translucent alpha tiers of cream (`rgba(247, 239, 228, x)`) to retain organic softness rather than stark white contrast.
3. **Flavor Accents:** Sage, Cocoa, Blush, and Rose are reserved specifically for culinary tags, product flavor variants, and illustrative gradients.

## Typography

The type system blends literary serif authority with modern humanist clarity:
- **Serif Voice (`Newsreader`):** Acts as the artisanal marquee. Its calligraphic weight transitions and gentle structural flourishes honor the culinary heritage without slipping into stiff formalist tropes. Hero statements pair italic emphasis for emotive ingredients or descriptions.
- **Sans-Serif Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean legibility for dense nutritional catalogs, store addresses, menus, and ordering funnels. Its rounded terminals harmonize with the warm shapes across the interface.

All serif headings employ negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain optical density and editorial refinement.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a centered 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at `1160px` (`72.5rem`), framed by fixed horizontal padding gutters of `24px` (`1.5rem`).

### Rhythm & Vertical Spacing
- **Base Rhythm:** Built on increments of `4px` and `8px`. Component internal padding leverages `10px` to `18px` steps to accommodate comfortable finger targets while preserving typographic density.
- **Section Spacing:** Major full-width thematic bands consistently use `88px` (`5.5rem`) vertical padding on desktop, reducing gracefully to `56px` (`3.5rem`) on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Behaviors
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stacks for editorial splits and store locator panes; 2-column compact grid for product/flavor cards; bottom navigation or minimal slide-out headers.
- **Tablet (640px - 859px):** 3-column product matrix layouts; search controls align horizontally; margins remain unified at `24px`.
- **Desktop (≥ 860px):** Asymmetric split layouts (such as `0.9fr / 1.1fr` content-to-visual ratios); persistent horizontal header rows; extended horizontal showcase strips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through warm tonal layering and atmospheric softness rather than cold drop shadows.

- **Warm Tonal Surfaces:** Elevation is primarily structural. Base body starts on cream neutral (`#F7EFE4`). Foreground cards lift up by shifting to clean paper tint (`#FFFBF5`) or recessed deep cream (`#EFE1CD`).
- **Atmospheric Halos:** Decorative depth is established using large, faint radial color fields—specifically warm orange (`rgba(222, 138, 76, 0.14)`) and soft plum (`rgba(124, 70, 103, 0.16)`) glowing subtly behind key products or hero visual clusters.
- **Physical Floats:** When interactive elements must physically project off the surface (such as slice selectors or interactive pastry boxes), use dual diffused berry-tinted shadows:
  - *Standard Card Lift:* `0 2px 8px rgba(42, 22, 32, 0.08)`
  - *Floating Slice / Drag Accent:* `0 10px 24px -6px rgba(42, 22, 32, 0.28)`
- **Low-Contrast Enclosures:** Darker plum containers rely on subtle boundary lines (`1px solid rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.16)`) and delicate internal backdrops (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.06)`) rather than stark boxed drop-shadow boundaries.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced, rounded shape language reflecting natural pastry silhouettes, hand-shaped dough, and baker's packaging.

- **Pill Geometry (`100px` / Full Radius):** Strictly reserved for dynamic interactive controls, primary action buttons, text input capsules, and delicate metadata tag badges.
- **Surface Containers (`16px` to `20px`):** Large visual sections, editorial feature tiles, and store locator modules use soft, generous corner radii (`1.25rem` / `20px`). Product catalog cards sit at `16px` (`1rem`).
- **Nested Media Shapes (`10px`):** Image boxes, inner card previews, and sub-elements nested inside outer containers step down their radius to maintain proportional concentricity.
- **Circular Badges (`50%`):** Reserved for media play indicators, rotation pivots, and status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), deep plum fill (`#432237`), cream text (`#F7EFE4`), and vertical padding of `12px 24px`. On hover, shift subtly upward with an orange accent highlight (`#DE8A4C`).
- **Secondary Interactive:** Full pill shape with terracotta orange fill (`#DE8A4C`), dark ink text (`#2A1620`), and font weight `500`.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Full pill shape with an organic border (`1px solid rgba(42, 22, 32, 0.2)`), transparent background, and berry ink text. On dark plum containers, switch the border and text to `rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.85)`.

### Chips & Flavor Badges
- Compact pill height with `6px 14px` padding.
- Eyebrow badges feature an inline `6px` circular terracotta dot preceding the text.
- Flavor variety chips apply low-saturation pastel tints (e.g., pistachio `#8B9B72` or rose `#C15D5E`) at `12%` background fill with a matching deep text tone.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Fully rounded capsule enclosure (`100px`) with minimum height of `48px`.
- On light backgrounds: Paper-white fill (`#FFFBF5`), `1px solid rgba(42, 22, 32, 0.15)`, and dark ink text.
- On dark plum containers: Translucent fill (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.1)`), translucent border (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.2)`), cream text, and soft cream placeholder (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.5)`). Focus rings use a gentle `2px` terracotta glow (`rgba(222, 138, 76, 0.5)`).

### Cards
- **Product / Flavor Cards:** Built on paper surfaces (`#FFFBF5`) with `16px` rounded corners and `18px` internal padding. Features a nested imagery container (`10px` radius) followed by serif heading and subdued body description.
- **Store Locator List Items:** Soft translucent container (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.05)`) with `14px` radius, hairline outline (`rgba(247, 239, 228, 0.12)`), and an orange accent callout for distance and availability.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes feature a `4px` corner radius; radio buttons are circular.
- Unchecked state uses a warm border (`1.5px solid rgba(42, 22, 32, 0.25)`). Checked state fills with deep plum (`#432237`) and presents a cream check icon.
