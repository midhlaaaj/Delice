Our Story

Started in a home kitchen, now boxed for all of Kerala.

What began as slow-baked cheesecakes shared among friends on quiet evenings turned into a dedicated team hand-cutting, packaging, and delivering fresh slices to boutique delis and neighborhood bakeries from Kozhikode to Kochi.

Each triangular portion is pre-sealed in custom food-grade packaging equipped with a miniature wooden spoon—ensuring every slice tastes as fresh and balanced as the moment it left our cooling racks.
